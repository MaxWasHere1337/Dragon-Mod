# Dragon-Mod
Dragon Mod for Minecraft (Bedrock Edition) for Madeline.

## What's in it so far (stage 1)
- **Forest Dragon** – lives in forests and jungles. Gentle; runs away if hurt.
- **Forest Dragon Egg** – found rarely in forests. Tap it ("Warm Egg") and it
  wobbles, then hatches into a baby after about 30 seconds.

### How to play
| To do this | Do this |
|---|---|
| Tame a dragon | Feed it apples, sweet berries or melon slices until hearts appear |
| Grow a baby faster | Feed it the same foods (otherwise it takes about 40 minutes) |
| Ride | Use a saddle on a tamed adult, then tap it to climb on |
| Fly | Push forward and look where you want to go; hold jump to rise |
| Land | Look down and fly to the ground, then sneak to hop off |
| Heal | Feed it apples, berries, melon or a golden apple |
| Get baby dragons | Feed two tamed adults glow berries or golden apples |

In Creative mode both the dragon and the egg have spawn eggs in the inventory.

## Installing
1. On a phone or tablet with Minecraft installed, download
   `dist/DragonMod.mcaddon` from this repository.
2. Open the downloaded file and choose Minecraft. It should say the import
   succeeded.
3. Create a new world. Under **Behavior Packs**, activate "Dragon Mod
   (Behavior)". The resource pack is switched on with it.
4. To play on a console, keep that world open on the phone/tablet and join it
   from the console (same Wi-Fi, or through the friends list).

## Rebuilding
`python3 tools/build.py` regenerates the models, textures and
`dist/DragonMod.mcaddon` (needs Python with Pillow).
