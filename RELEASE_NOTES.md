First test version: the **Forest Dragon**.

- Find Forest Dragons and Forest Dragon Eggs in forests and jungles
- Tap an egg to warm it and hatch a baby dragon
- Tame dragons with apples, sweet berries or melon slices
- Put a saddle on a tamed adult, climb on and fly

**To install:** download `DragonMod.mcaddon` below and open it with Minecraft
(Bedrock Edition, version 1.21.90 or newer). Then turn on "Dragon Mod
(Behavior)" under Behavior Packs when creating a world.

This is an early alpha and has not been play-tested yet, so expect rough edges.
